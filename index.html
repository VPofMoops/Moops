About Moops

Moops is a country that has its own currency and military. It is governed by a President and four other high-ranking officials: the Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Secretary of Commerce.

Military

The leader of the army, or Fleet Admiral, is Hunter. The military is currently at war with the Muzz clan (Turkish for banana).

Treasury

The treasury hasn’t been able to give people pay-day in cash, because it is a long and grueling process that neither the Treasurer nor the Vice President wants to do. The treasury currently holds 120k moops, thanks to the 2 mooper (20k) and the 10 mooper (100k).

Credit cards are now available in the United Kingdom of Moopsioid, which has been a great addition because they make transferring currency so easy. There are four types of credit cards: Yellow, Red, Blue, and White. Each has a different interest rate and a different way of earning it.
